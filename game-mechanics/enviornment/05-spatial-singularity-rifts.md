# Spec Sheet: Spatial Singularity Rifts (ID: 05)

## 1. Overview & Classification
* **Environmental Category:** Macro-Scale Space-Time Anomaly (High-Risk Strategic Zone & High-Tier Resource Node)
* **Classification:** Repulsive Spatial Singularity / Core Anomaly with Spacetime Expansion Field
* **Mobility Status:** Stationary macro-anomaly with consistent outward force dynamics.

## 2. Spatial Scale & Form Factor
* **Core Size:** Compact footprint equivalent to the physical size of a Flagship.
* **Repulsion Field Radius:** Active gravitational push effect extends outward in a precise **200-meter radius** from the core.
* **Radar & Telemetry Rules:** Does *not* create a radar shadow or blind sensor arrays. Fleet telemetry and radar tracking remain fully operational across the anomaly, allowing full visibility of units operating inside the zone.

## 3. Physics & Repulsion Mechanics
* **Repulsion Wave:** Any standard unit or projectile that enters the 200m radius is forcefully and continuously pushed outward away from the core until it clears the boundary.
* **Weight-Class Influence:** The outward push actively impacts **3W and lighter units**, forcing them away from the anomaly and disrupting their pathfinding vectors. Heavier assets experience heavy forward resistance when moving toward the core.
* **The Grav Extractor Exception:** **Grav Extractors (2W)** are specifically engineered to interface with these anomalies, operating as the *only* free-moving units capable of holding position and navigating directly inside the 200m range without being repelled.

## 4. Ballistic & Projectile Refraction
* **Trajectory Alteration:** Projectiles shot into or across the 200m range have their trajectories bent or deflected at a **precise and predictable angle** based on the vector of entry, creating a natural kinetic redirection hazard or tactical ricochet shield.

## 5. Resource Extraction & Logistics Slingshot Loop
* **Primary Resources:** **Graviton Cores (Resource Type 5)**—high-priority, low-volume energy-mass catalysts extracted directly from the core anomaly.
* **Assigned Harvesting Vessel:** **Grav Extractors (2W)**.
* **Logistics Rendezvous & Slingshot Loop:** Because radar is fully functional, Supply Tenders (3W) can pathfind directly into the zone. The Tender fights its way inward against the outward force to dock with the Grav Extractor. Once loading is complete, the singularity's active repulsion field acts as a high-speed launchpad, slingshotting the Supply Tender out of the radius much faster than its own engines can achieve alone.
