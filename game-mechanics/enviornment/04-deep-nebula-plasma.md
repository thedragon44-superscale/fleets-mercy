# Spec Sheet: Deep Nebula Plasma Fields (ID: 04)

## 1. Spatial Scale & Form Factor
* **Map-Dependent Architecture:** The size, shape, and distribution of Deep Nebula Plasma fields are entirely custom and dependent on specific map designs, forming natural tactical lanes, pockets, or cloud corridors.
* **Radar Mapping:** The physical boundaries and accurate shape of the nebula are clearly rendered and trackable on player radar and HUD interfaces.

## 2. Radar Suppression & Vision Mechanics
* **Inside Blindspot:** Radar is completely blind to units physically located *inside* the nebula's boundaries; enemy units within the cloud cannot be detected by standard fleet telemetry.
* **External & Trans-Nebula Range:** Radar arrays and Recon Probes can accurately track objects outside or past the nebula as long as their range extends beyond the cloud boundary. The nebula only conceals what resides *within* its volume.
* **Visual Range Reduction:** Standard visual and unassisted sensor ranges for units operating within or looking into the nebula are cut in half (e.g., standard 600m visual ranges drop to 300m).

## 3. Physics & Movement Modifiers
* **Inertial Drag for Light-to-Medium Assets:** Due to the dense plasma particulate, **3W and lighter units** experience a proportional decrease in both movement speed and agility.
* **The Skimmer Exception:** **Plasma Skimmers (2W)**—engineered with magnetic vacuum intake scoops and specialized shielding designed for volatile gas harvesting and environmental radiation—remain completely unaffected by the nebula's movement penalties.
* **Heavier Assets:** Capital ships and heavy components (4W and 5W) maintain their standard movement profiles, cutting straight through the particulate.

## 4. Health & Shield Integrity
* **Zero Environmental Damage:** The plasma nebula does *not* apply continuous damage over time to hulls or shields, posing purely tactical, vision, and navigational challenges.

## 5. Resource Harvesting & Logistics Loop
* **Primary Resources:** Rich source for **Plasma Gas (Resource Type 4)** and **Isotopes (Resource Type 3)** found in volatile nebulae and radiation clouds.
* **Harvesting Vessel:** Harvested by specialized **Plasma Skimmers (2W)** operating without speed penalties.
* **Logistics Rendezvous Protocol:** To avoid blind searching inside the cloud, Supply Tenders (3W) hover just *inside* the outer perimeter of the nebula to gain radar-blind safety cover. When a skimmer's hold reaches 100% capacity, it zips out of the deep pocket at full unhindered speed to meet the tender at the edge for a safe offload.
