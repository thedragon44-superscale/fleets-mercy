# Spec Sheet: Major Worlds & Terrestrial Crusts (ID: 01)

## 1. Overview & Classification
* **Environmental Category:** Unified Macro-Landmark (Static Strategic Terrain & Primary Resource Node)[cite: 18]
* **Classification:** Terrestrial Planet / Rocky World with Tectonic Crust & Subterranean Network[cite: 18]
* **Mobility Status:** 100% Stationary (Fixed macro-scale celestial body)[cite: 18].

## 2. Macro-Scale Physics & Gravity Well
* **Spatial Scale:** Massive macro-footprint with a standard radius of **1,000 to 1,500 world units** (approximately 15–20 times the length of a flagship), designed to dominate regional sectors on a 12,000-unit baseline tactical map.
* **Pathfinding Barrier:** Absolute physical boundary. Fleet units cannot pass *through* the planet; they must route around its curvature or orbit its trajectory[cite: 18].
* **Gravitational Well:** Exerts a heavy gravitational pull that alters ship acceleration, turn rates, and warp vector calculations when passing through its proximity envelope[cite: 18].
* **Sensor Transparency:** Planets do *not* create radar shadows or blind sensors. Telemetry and absolute position tracking remain fully visible regardless of planetary positioning[cite: 18].

## 3. Surface Crust, Sub-Surface Tunnels, & Economic Harvesting
* **Topography:** Features deep surface fault lines, impact basins, and a network of internal subterranean caverns and tunnels containing high-yield mineral deposits[cite: 18].
* **Primary Harvested Resources:** **Metal** and **Ore**[cite: 18].
* **Assigned Harvesting Vessel:** Mining Barges (sole extractors)[cite: 18].
* **Sub-Surface & Logistics Roles:**
  * **Mining Barges:** Pathfind into planetary caverns and tunnel networks to extract resources. They are physically separated from direct combat once underground[cite: 18].
  * **Supply Tenders:** Drop in exclusively to dock with harvesting vessels, pick up resource batches, and immediately depart. They do not harvest nodes themselves[cite: 18].
  * **Combat Ships:** Combat units (corvettes, frigates, etc.) are structurally unsuited for tight planetary geology and remain in open space/upper atmosphere[cite: 18].

## 4. Weapon Immunity, Targeting Rules, & Destruction Mechanics
* **Light Ammo Immunity:** Light primary weapons, auto-cannons, and infinite-ammo light munitions register **0 damage** to the planet or its rock formations. Landmasses cannot be chipped away or degraded by standard weapon spam[cite: 18].
* **Automated AI Targeting Restriction:** Automated units strictly follow preset AI target directives (they target *units only*). They cannot intentionally attack ground or target neutral cave mouths[cite: 18].
* **The Flagship Exception:** Because the flagship is player-operated, it bypasses automated AI restrictions and *can* intentionally target and fire heavy payloads at destructible land formations to trigger a collapse[cite: 18].
* **Collateral Environmental Damage:** For automated units, a cavern or node only becomes vulnerable to collateral damage when economic units are operating inside or near it, drawing enemy fire where missed heavy shots can cause a structural cave-in[cite: 18].
* **Permanent Consequences:** Once a resource node or tunnel system is collapsed (via flagship command or heavy collateral fire), it is **permanently destroyed for the remainder of the match**, cutting off that economic pipeline forever along with any units trapped inside[cite: 18].

## 5. Logistics & Tactical Gameplay Loop
* **Logistical Route:** Supply Tenders briefly cycle between active surface/subterranean nodes and command nodes to haul raw metal and ore batches[cite: 18].
* **The Flagship Siege Dynamic:** Enemy player-operated flagships can hover in space or the upper atmosphere above a cavern entrance to ambush incoming logistics or intentionally blast the cave shut to permanently deny resources[cite: 18].
