# Master Environmental Spec Sheet: Tactical Hazards & Celestial Bodies

## 1. Major Worlds & Terrestrial Crusts (ID: 01)
* **Category:** Unified Macro-Landmark (Static Strategic Terrain & Primary Resource Node)[cite: 7]
* **Scale & Physics:** Standard radius of **1,000 to 1,500 units** (15-20x a flagship)[cite: 7]. Absolute pathfinding barrier; exerts a heavy gravitational pull that alters ship acceleration, turn rates, and warp vectors[cite: 7]. Radar-transparent[cite: 7].
* **Economy:** Contains deep subterranean fault lines and tunnels yielding **Metal** and **Ore**, extracted exclusively by **Mining Barges**[cite: 7]. Supply Tenders dock with harvesters; combat units remain in open space[cite: 7].
* **Combat & Destruction:** Light weapons register 0 damage[cite: 7]. Automated AI cannot target ground nodes[cite: 7]. Flagship-controlled players can fire heavy payloads at cave mouths to trigger permanent structural collapses, permanently cutting off the economic pipeline and trapping units inside[cite: 7].

## 2. Drifting Mineral Planetoids (ID: 02)
* **Category:** Dynamic Macro-Landmark (Moving Strategic Terrain & Secondary Resource Node)[cite: 8]
* **Scale & Physics:** Slow, constant drift across map coordinates[cite: 8]. Radius of **300 to 600 units**[cite: 8]. Acts as a solid physical barrier blocking projectiles and direct-fire LoS; exerts a weak localized gravity well[cite: 8]. Radar-transparent[cite: 8].
* **Economy:** Exposed surface crust craters and open fissures packed with ores and rare crystals[cite: 8]. Harvested by Mining Barges directly on the surface, leaving them completely vulnerable to enemy fire[cite: 8].
* **Durability:** Permanently indestructible to optimize CPU performance; resource veins can be economically exhausted, but collision meshes remain static[cite: 8].

## 3. Gas Giant Atmospheres (ID: 03)
* **Category:** Macro-Scale Planetary Landmark (Volatile Hazard Zone & Resource Node)[cite: 9]
* **Scale & Envelopes:** Standard radius of **3,000 to 4,000 units**[cite: 9]. Divided into three concentric layers:
  * **The Core (0%–60%):** Instant death zone; zero wreckage recoverable[cite: 9].
  * **Deep Atmosphere (60%–85%):** Automated units refuse to enter. 1W units are pulled relentlessly toward the core; 2W+ units suffer severe movement/agility penalties and continuous shield/hull damage[cite: 9]. Provides complete radar and telemetry invisibility[cite: 9].
  * **Outer Atmosphere (85%–100%):** Safe for transit and skimming[cite: 9]. 1W units experience zero drag; 2W+ experience minor movement drag[cite: 9].
* **Economy:** Rich in volatile gases, heavy hydrogen, and plasma/fuel resources[cite: 9]. Harvested via specialized vessels entirely exposed to enemy raid lines[cite: 9].

## 4. Deep Nebula Plasma Fields (ID: 04)
* **Category:** Custom tactical hazard lanes and cloud corridors[cite: 10].
* **Vision & Radar:** Radar is completely blind to units physically inside the cloud[cite: 10]. Radar arrays/probes can track objects *outside* or *past* the nebula[cite: 10]. Visual and unassisted sensor ranges inside the cloud are cut in half[cite: 10].
* **Physics:** 3W and lighter units experience inertial drag (decreased speed and agility)[cite: 10]. **Plasma Skimmers (2W)** are completely immune to movement penalties due to magnetic shielding[cite: 10]. Zero environmental damage over time[cite: 10].
* **Economy:** Primary source for **Plasma Gas (Type 4)** and **Isotopes (Type 3)**[cite: 10]. Supply Tenders park just inside the perimeter to receive offloads from Skimmers operating safely in deep pockets[cite: 10].

## 5. Spatial Singularity Rifts (ID: 05)
* **Category:** Macro-Scale Space-Time Anomaly[cite: 11]
* **Scale & Physics:** Compact core footprint with a precise **200m active repulsion field**[cite: 11]. Fully radar-transparent[cite: 11]. Forces 3W and lighter units outward; heavier units face forward resistance[cite: 11]. **Grav Extractors (2W)** are uniquely immune and can hold position inside[cite: 11].
* **Ballistics:** Refracts/deflects incoming projectiles at precise, predictable angles[cite: 11].
* **Economy:** Yields high-priority **Graviton Cores (Resource Type 5)** harvested by Grav Extractors[cite: 11]. Supply Tenders brave the inbound pull to dock, and use the anomaly's outward repulsion field as a high-speed launchpad slingshot[cite: 11].

## 6. Moons & Satellites (ID: 06)
* **Category:** Secondary Macro-Landmark (Orbital Strategic Terrain)[cite: 12]
* **Scale & Physics:** Slow, predictable orbital motion around parent bodies[cite: 12]. Acts as a solid physical pathfinding and line-of-sight barrier[cite: 12]. Radar-transparent (network telemetry tracks positions even when visually blocked)[cite: 12].
* **Archetypes:** Split into *Resource-Bearing Moons* (harvested by Mining Barges) and *Barren Moons* (pure geography/cover)[cite: 12]. Permanently indestructible for CPU optimization[cite: 12].

## 7. Dense Asteroid Belts (ID: 07)
* **Category:** Stationary Navigational Terrain[cite: 13]
* **Scale & Physics:** Solid physical barrier providing custom tactical corridors[cite: 13]. Only **1W and 2W light units** can slip through the dense clusters; 3W+ units must route around[cite: 13]. Fully radar-transparent[cite: 13].
* **Durability:** Permanently indestructible; cannot be mined or destroyed by payloads[cite: 13].

## 9. Comets (ID: 09)
* **Category:** High-Speed Transient Interstellar Anomaly[cite: 14]
* **Scale & Dynamics:** Small 1W physical mass crossing the map rapidly from edge to edge (5 to 9 randomized appearances per match)[cite: 14].
* **Impact Scaling:** Instantly melts 1W/2W units; heavily damages 3W/4W units; absorbed by 5W capital shields (causing hull damage if shields are down)[cite: 14].
* **AI & Tactics:** Broadcasts trajectory on radar instantly; automated units attempt evasion[cite: 14]. Players can dodge or strategically herd enemies into its path[cite: 14].
