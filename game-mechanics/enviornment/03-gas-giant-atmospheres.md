# Spec Sheet: Gas Giant Atmospheres (ID: 03)

## 1. Overview & Classification
* **Environmental Category:** Macro-Scale Planetary Landmark (Volatile Hazard Zone & Resource Node)
* **Classification:** Super-Giant Gas Planet with Layered Atmospheric Gradient and Dense Core
* **Mobility Status:** 100% Stationary (Fixed macro-scale celestial body).

## 2. Spatial Scale & Proximity Envelope
* **Spatial Scale:** Massive macro-footprint with a total standard radius of **3,000 to 4,000 world units**, dominating an entire quadrant of the 12,000-unit baseline map.
* **Physical Block:** The core and upper cloud decks act as an absolute physical barrier, blocking projectiles, direct-fire weapons, and visual line-of-sight.

## 3. Concentric Atmospheric Zones & Physics
The gas giant is divided into three distinct concentric layers from the center outward:

* **Layer 1: The Core (0% to 60% Radius) — Unnavigable / Death Zone**
  * **Lethality:** The extreme pressure gradient and dense core make this zone completely unnavigable. Any unit (flagship, corvette, or harvester) that makes direct contact is **instantly and completely destroyed** with zero wreckage recoverable.

* **Layer 2: The Deep Atmosphere (60% to 85% Radius) — The Tactical Hazard Shroud**
  * **AI Autonomy Rule:** Automated, AI-controlled units will *never* willingly pathfind into this hazard zone. Only a player-controlled flagship can override this and anchor or steer squads into it.
  * **Physics & Gravity Impact:** 
    * **1W (Lightweight) Units:** Too lightweight to resist current forces, 1W units are relentlessly pulled inward toward the 60% core boundary until destroyed, unless tethered or pulled out.
    * **2W+ and Heavier Units:** Resist the center-pull entirely, but suffer **severe reductions in movement speed and agility**.
  * **Damage Trade-Off:** Units linger at the cost of continuous environmental damage applied directly to shields (and exposed hull if shields are depleted).
  * **Sensor & Stealth Shield:** Any unit operating within this deep hazard zone is **completely invisible to radar and fleet telemetry arrays**, trading absolute stealth against constant structural degradation.

* **Layer 3: The Outer Atmosphere (85% to 100% Radius) — The Skimming Zone**
  * **Transit & Safety:** Safe for general transit and specialized harvesting vessels.
  * **Drag Mechanics:** 
    * **1W Units:** High thrust-to-weight ratios allow them to slice through the outer edge with **zero drag or penalty**.
    * **2W+ and Heavier Units:** Experience **minor atmospheric drag**, slowing travel speeds slightly without any center-pull.

## 4. Resource Extraction & Economic Harvesting
* **Primary Harvested Resources:** Volatiles, heavy hydrogen, and plasma/fuel resources.
* **Assigned Harvesting Vessel:** Specialized harvesters or adapted supply/mining vessels.
* **Vulnerability:** Lacking subterranean tunnels or surface crusts, harvesting units operating in the outer atmosphere are entirely exposed to enemy line-of-sight and raids, balancing high-yield fuel extraction with high operational risk.
