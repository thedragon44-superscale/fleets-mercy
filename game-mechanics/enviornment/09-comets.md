# Spec Sheet: Comets (ID: 09)

## 1. Overview & Classification
* **Environmental Category:** Transient Hazard / Dynamic Interstellar Anomaly
* **Classification:** High-Velocity Minor Celestial Body / Comet
* **Mobility Status:** **High-Speed Transient Motion** (Randomized linear trajectory crossing the map rapidly from edge to edge with zero persistent environmental residue or trailing debris).

## 2. Appearance & Spawning Dynamics
* **Spatial Scale:** Small physical mass comparable to the size of a 1W vessel, accompanied by a glowing, ephemeral tail.
* **Frequency & Timing:** Randomized appearances throughout an individual match (averaging 5 to 9 appearances per game), creating unpredictable timing windows and ticking-clock scenarios.
* **Trajectory:** Completely random trajectory upon spawning, making vectors impossible to predict until the comet actively registers on radar.

## 3. Impact & Weight-Class Damage Scaling
When a comet tears through a unit's coordinates, destructive force scales strictly by structural weight class:
* **1W & 2W Units:** Instantly **melted and destroyed** on impact.
* **3W & 4W Units:** Suffers **heavy damage** directly to shields and exposed hulls, severely crippling or destroying unprepared assets.
* **5W Units (Capital Class):** Capable of absorbing the kinetic impact utilizing their massive shielding. However, 5W units with already-compromised or cracked shields will take **moderate hull damage**.

## 4. Automated AI Evasion & Behavioral Protocols
* **Radar Detection:** Comets appear on radar and fleet telemetry the moment they enter map bounds, broadcasting their trajectory.
* **Automated Evasion:** Automated, AI-controlled units will actively detect incoming comets via the fleet array and attempt to evade collision paths.
* **Behavioral Variance:** Evasion maneuvers are not uniform; individual units react and scatter slightly differently based on their specific pre-programmed AI behavior protocols and movement capabilities.

## 5. Tactical Utilization & Player Agency
* **Offensive Positioning:** Because trajectories are visible upon radar appearance, alert players can actively choose to dodge the hazard or strategically maneuver their heavy assets (or herd enemies) to use the comet's devastating kinetic path as an offensive weapon.
