Unit Specs: Specter Radar Jammer (3W)
Role: Squad-Wide Electronic Concealment, Radar Disruption & Stealth Support

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: Rank A / Hull: Rank C)

Health Class: Rank A Shields / Rank C Hull (Unified Pool).

Durability & Defense Philosophy: Heavily shielded (Rank A) to absorb counter-intel tracing, but built with a fragile baseline hull (Rank C). If shields drop, it is extremely vulnerable.

Retreat Protocol: Follows standard fleet retreat rules, disengaging to seek the nearest active port or flagship drydock when compromised.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank B - Standard Frigate Speed)

Speed Class: Rank B (Moderate Acceleration / Standard Mobility).

3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry Interaction

Jamming vs. Relay Role: Replaces standard open-relay functionality with active stealth projection. Suppresses enemy telemetry and masks signature returns within its operational footprint.

Detection Range: Standard frigate-tier range (600m).

4. Utility Systems & Resource Mechanics (Shield-Gated Cloaking Array)

Primary System: Aetherium Electronic Cloaking Array. Requires no resource upkeep.

The Shield-Gated Stealth & Distance Rule: Grants the exact radar invisibility of the 1W Phantom unit to its squad, subject to strict operational parameters:

Formation Proximity: Squadmates must remain within the Specter's standard squad formation distance (to be precisely balanced in macro-development) to maintain the cloak. If a unit strays too far out of formation, they drop out of the jamming field and become visible on radar.

The >75% Shield Threshold: The jammer only functions completely when the Specter's shields are above 75%.

Disruption Loop: Taking fire drops shields below 75%, causing the squad-wide stealth to flicker and shut down immediately. The cloak remains offline until the Specter breaks combat long enough for its shields to fully regenerate back above 75%.

5. AI Loop & Squad Mechanics

Squad Integration & Behavior: Functions identically to a support repair vessel. It never dictates or leads the squad's directive; instead, it matches pace, sticking closely behind its squadmates to passively blanket them in invisibility while they execute their primary orders.
