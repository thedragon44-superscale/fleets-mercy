Unit Specs: Cryo-Flak Frigate (3W)
Role: Anti-Swarm Area Denial, Tactical Interdiction, and Thermal/Ordnance Suppression

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: Rank B / Hull: Rank B)

Health Class: Rank B Shields / Rank B Hull (Unified Pool).

Durability & Positioning: Balanced medium-heavy survivability designed to anchor defensive choke points or screen high-value support lines.

Retreat Protocol: Follows standard fleet retreat rules, disengaging to seek the nearest active port or flagship drydock when compromised.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank B - Standard Frigate Speed)

Speed Class: Rank B (Moderate Acceleration / Standard Mobility). Pivots smoothly to realign its flak batteries against shifting attack vectors.

3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry Interaction

Radar Relay Role: Doubles as a passive radar relay for the fleet sensor array, extending telemetry and fog-of-war visibility.

Detection Range & Engagement Limit: Strict operational range tied to its field of vision (600m max range).

4. Utility Systems & Resource Mechanics (Ordnance Output: Rank B / Finite Cryo Ammunition)

Primary System: Cryo-Flak Batteries.

Targeting & Range: Limited strictly to its 600m visual field. Fires precision proximity shells that detonate to lace a 75m radius with heavy cryogenic gas clouds.

Specialized Gas Effects & Duration:

Duration: The detonated cryo gas is temporary, wearing off completely after 10 seconds.

Anti-Swarm Lethality: Instantly melts and shreds fragile 1W units caught within the active gas cloud.

Armor Penetration: Bypasses shields entirely, inflicting Rank C hull damage directly to targets inside the cloud.

Kinetic & Thermal Sabotage: Severely slows enemy movement speed and increases cooldown times / accelerates heat accumulation for any units relying on cooldown or heat-management systems.

Ordnance Detonation: Any incoming enemy torpedoes or unguided munitions that enter the cryo gas cloud detonate immediately on contact with the gas.

Payload & Resupply: Uses finite ammunition manufactured from a mix of metal and volatile gas, resupplied by Supply Tenders.

5. AI Loop & Squad Mechanics

Squad Integration: Operates as an escort frigate or localized area-denial anchor.

Screening AI Loop: Automatically calculates trajectories of incoming swarms, torpedoes, and fast movers within its 600m visual zone, blanketing transit lanes with temporary cryo gas to disrupt enemy timing and protect friendly assets.
