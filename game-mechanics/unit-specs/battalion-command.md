Unit Specs: Battalion Command Ship (5W)
Role: Regional Fleet Command, Mobile Repair Hub, & Capital Artillery Platform

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Quadrant Shielding / Rank S Hull / External Repair Only)

Health Class & Architecture: Features Quadrant Shielding (Forward, Port, Starboard, Aft) with localized hull damage calculation, matching the Flagship design.

Repair Limitation: It cannot repair itself. Capital-tier maintenance requires external field intervention; it must be serviced by deployed Aegis Repair units (2W). (The Flagship shares this exact limitation).

Durability Profile: Massive capital-grade hull (Rank S) reinforced by heavy energy screening per quadrant, vulnerable to specialised sniper units (Lancers) and Flagships once shields are breached.

Retreat Protocol: Enters a heavy emergency withdrawal status when critically compromised, seeking secure friendly territory.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank C - Heavy Capital Cruising Speed & Quadrant Failure Penalties)

Speed Class: Rank C (Massive Inertia / Slow Acceleration). Relies on Command Escort Ships (3W) to cover vulnerable vectors.

Aft Damage Penalty: If the rear/aft quadrant is damaged, the ship's propulsion and stabilization systems are compromised, meaning it cannot properly strafe or thrust.

3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry Interaction

Active Command Relay: Functions as a major active radar and telemetry relay, broadcasting a wide sensor bubble across the regional map zone to pierce the fog-of-war.

Detection Range: Extended capital-tier telemetry range (1200m+).

4. Utility Systems & Resource Mechanics (Weapon Parity, Metal-Only Logistics, & Quadrant Dependencies)

Primary System: Capital Battery Array, Integrated Automated Drydock Bays, and Docking Ports.

Weapon Parity: Shares the exact same weapon loadout, firing mechanics, and attack range as the Flagship.

Weapon Firing Mechanic & Front Quadrant Dependency:

Shield Drop on Fire: The front shield momentarily drops when firing its main cannon.

Front Damage Penalty: If the front quadrant is damaged, the main weapon is disabled entirely and cannot fire.

Logistics Scope (Metal Only): Dedicated strictly to structural upkeep using metal as the sole resource. It does not handle ammunition.

Port & Bay Damage Dependencies:

Port Damage: If the port docking sector is damaged/destroyed, it cannot dock units needing repair or accept supply vessels dropping off metal.

Deploy Bay Damage: If the deployment bay is damaged, it cannot deploy units out into the field.

5. AI Loop & Command Behavior

Escort & Repair Integration: Automatically pairs with assigned Command Escort Ships (3W) for physical screening, while relying on external Aegis Repair units for structural restoration.

Strategic AI Loop: Balances heavy capital combat using Flagship-equivalent weaponry with positional stability, managing the automated repair queue and anchoring regional operations.
