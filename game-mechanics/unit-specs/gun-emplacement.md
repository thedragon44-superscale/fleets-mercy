Unit Specs: Defensive Gun Emplacement (4W)
Role: Stationary Strategic Bunker, Chokepoint Denial, & Territory Fortification

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: None / Hull: Rank S)

Health Class: Zero Shields / Rank S Hull (Unified Pool). Completely devoid of energy shielding; relies entirely on massive, impenetrable structural armor (Rank S) to absorb punishment.

Deployment & Transit Mechanics:

Recon Anchor Routing: Requires a designated 1W Recon drone anchor to set its travel destination.

Evasive Pathfinding: During transit, it takes the safest route, actively avoiding enemy fleets and leveraging environmental cover.

Landmark Binding: Upon arrival, it must physically attach and bolt onto the nearest available landmark (such as an asteroid, moon, planet, or mining location).

Movement Profile: Stateless/Immobile once anchored.

Retreat Protocol: None. Cannot retreat to a drydock; fights until destroyed, leaving behind salvageable wreckage for supply ships.

2. Vision, Radar & Telemetry Interaction

Radar Relay Role: Acts as a static passive radar relay for the fleet sensor array, anchoring the sensor grid around its fortified landmark.

Engagement Range: Hard-capped firing and engagement range of 1000m. Relies on the broader fleet network for targets outside its line of sight, but will not fire until targets enter its 1000m bubble.

3. Utility Systems & Resource Mechanics (Ordnance Output: Rank A / Rank B Fire Rate / Graviton-Core & Ore Ammunition)

Primary System: Fixed Heavy Artillery / Point-Defense Batteries. (No friendly fire applies).

Fire Rate & Thermal Management:

Fire Rate: Rank B standard ballistic/energy release cadence.

Sustained Fire Penalty: If it continuously fires for 10 seconds, it undergoes an automatic barrel-cooling or thermal-cycling cycle, resulting in a short 3-second cooldown period before it can resume firing.

Ammunition & Supply Logistics: Deploys with a massive, cheap initial finite ammunition cache. Refilling depleted magazines requires Supply Tenders delivering a specialized mix of graviton cores and ore.

Combat Capabilities: Shreds advancing medium units, zones out light swarms, and punishes unsupported strike craft entering its 1000m arc.

4. AI Loop & Combat Behavior

Targeting Priority: Automatically locks onto enemy units entering its 1000m firing arc, prioritizing high-threat entities threatening its anchored territory.

Stationary Defense Loop: Maintains constant watch, managing its 10-second sustained fire cycles and graviton-ore ordnance until depleted or serviced by a Supply Tender.
