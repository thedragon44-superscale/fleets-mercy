Unit Specs: Assault Gunship (2W)
Role: High-Speed Subsystem Strafing, Logistics Interdiction & Escort Harassment

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: Rank C / Hull: Rank C)

Health Class: Rank C Shields / Rank C Hull (Unified Pool).

Durability: Built with a heavy twin-engine bow and reinforced forward bulk plating designed to absorb head-on momentum during aggressive strafing runs.

Shield-Break Retreat Protocol: The moment its unified pool's shields drop completely (shield break), it instantly aborts its attack vector, triggers emergency thrusters, and executes a mandatory retreat back to the nearest active repair vessel or port vessel for shield recharging and hull maintenance. Speed Class: Rank A (High Speed / High Acceleration).

Inertial Strafe Loop: Utilizes vector-thrust bow engines to execute high-velocity hit-and-run passes, sliding laterally while maintaining forward firing arcs to sweep past enemy lines without losing momentum.

3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry Interaction

Detection Range: Standard visual and telemetry range (600m), syncing with Recon arrays to pinpoint high-speed intersection vectors.

4. Utility Systems & Resource Mechanics (Kinetic Output: Rank C / Infinite Supply)

Primary System: Twin Rotary Kinetic Cannons.

Ammunition & Resource Independence: Infinite C-Rank ammo. It requires zero resources, supply loops, or ammunition restocking.

Damage Profile: Rank C continuous kinetic damage optimized for harassing enemy logistics, stripping surface sub-components, and wearing down isolated targets. (No friendly fire applies).

5. AI Loop & Squad Mechanics

Squad Integration: Operates within standard combat squads and battalions as a rapid-response harassment unit.

Strafing AI Loop: Executes high-velocity vector passes—accelerating into range, unleashing rotary kinetic fire, and looping away. Governed strictly by its shield-break rule to disengage and seek port/repair vessels the second its defensive buffer fails.
