Unit Specs: Lancer Railgun Corvette (4W)
Role: Long-Range Anti-Armor Flagship & Strategic Asset Sniper

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: Rank C / Hull: Rank C)

Health Class: Rank C Shields / Rank C Hull (Unified Pool).

Durability: Extended, hyper-streamlined needle chassis housing its massive linear acceleration rails. Built for long-range distance preservation rather than close-quarters brawling.

Retreat Protocol: Follows standard fleet retreat rules. When its health drops to critical thresholds, it breaks position and returns to the nearest active port vessel for repairs. (Note: Port vessels consume metal to execute repairs and must be periodically supplied by the logistics network).

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank C - Moderate)

Speed Class: Rank C (Medium Speed). Continuously micro-adjusts position to maintain clean line-of-sight on high-value targets while holding maximum standoff distance.

3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry Interaction

Detection Range: Extended sniper targeting range, utilizing Fleet Array and Recon telemetry to calculate firing solutions far beyond standard visual bounds.

4. Utility Systems & Resource Mechanics (Railgun Output: Rank S / Armor Piercing: Rank S)

Primary System: Heavy Sub-Atomic Railgun Array.

Ammunition Cache & Composition:

Comes equipped with an initial cache of 10 Heavy Railgun Slugs.

Slugs are manufactured from a specialized blend of Plasma, Metal, and Exotic Crystals.

Logistics Resupply Loop: When its slug cache is depleted, it must convene directly with a Supply Unit in the field (which holds the necessary resources to manufacture the ammo), rather than requiring a mandatory return all the way to the Flagship.

Damage Profile: Rank S single-target piercing damage designed to punch straight through major capital plating. (No friendly fire applies).

5. AI Loop, Flagship Anchoring & Strategic Targeting

Flagship Exclusive Anchor: The Lancer cannot be assigned to a standard combat squad or battalion. It is structurally tethered exclusively to the Flagship's command telemetry.

Manual Radial Deployment: Cannot be deployed via bulk squad commands; the player must use the individual unit radial deployment wheel to launch it from the Flagship's bays.

Strict High-Value Targeting Matrix: The Lancer's automated targeting AI completely ignores standard combat fodder, restricting its firing solutions exclusively to:

Flagships

Battalion Command Vessels

Resource Harvesters

Supply Units

Recon Units

Warp Units
