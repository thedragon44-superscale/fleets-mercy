Unit Specs: Supply Tender (2W)
Role: Mobile Munitions Synthesizer, Field Fabricator & Logistics Courier

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Hull: Rank B / Shields: Rank C)

Health Class: Rank B Hull / Rank C Shields (Unified Pool).

Durability: Reinforced containment frame to safely transport raw energetic materials and volatile fabricated ordnance.

Retreat Protocol: If its shields are completely broken (0%), it immediately aborts operations and retreats to the Flagship's (or Battalion Command Ship's) Port Docking Bay for repairs.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank C - Moderate Velocity)

Speed Class: Rank C (Balanced speed to maintain efficient transit cycles between field Harvesters and active combat lines).

Agility: Moderate acceleration and vectoring capabilities for smooth mid-space docking/tethering.

3. Vision, Radar & Fleet Array Interaction

Detection Range: Standard visual range (600m).

Fleet Logistics Telemetry: Uses the Fleet Array to continuously monitor:

Raw resource cache levels inside active Harvester holds.

Depletion levels of finite secondary munitions, missiles, and specialty ordnance across all deployed combat units.

4. Manufacturing & Resupply Systems (Synthesis: Rank S / Combat Dmg: Rank D)

Primary System: On-Board Rapid Ordnance Synthesizer & Wireless Fabrication Tether.

On-Demand Manufacturing: Does not carry pre-made infinite ammo. Instead, it converts raw resources harvested in the field directly into specific finite ammunition and secondary payloads required by nearby friendly ships.

Resupply Speed: Rank S (Instantly synthesizes and transfers payloads once in tether range).

Combat Damage: Rank D (Unarmed).

5. AI Loop & Field Logistics Network

Field Logistics Loop:

Resource Collection: Paths to the nearest Harvester with raw resources in its cache and transfers the materials into its internal synthesis hold.

Target Matching: Scans the Fleet Array for the nearest friendly combat unit requiring secondary/finite ammunition that matches its current raw resource inventory.

Field Delivery: Vectors directly to the depleted combat unit, synthesizes the specific ordnance required on-the-fly, and transfers it via tether beam.

Cycle Continuation: Returns to nearby Harvesters for additional materials as long as field demand remains.

Evasive Behavior: Avoids direct line-of-fire. If threatened, uses obstacle cover and maneuvers to position friendly combat units between itself and hostiles.
