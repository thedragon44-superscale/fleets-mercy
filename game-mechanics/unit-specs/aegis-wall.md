Unit Specs: Aegis Wall (5W)Role: Projected Directional Shield Barrier, Super-Heavy Vanguard & Battalion Linchpin   
1. Vitality & Mitigation (Front Shield: Rank S / Hull & Rear: Rank D)Health Class: Rank S Directional Shield / Rank D Hull & Rear Shields.Barrier Mechanics: The 180-degree forward barrier functions identically to standard fleet shields, but is scaled to super-heavy capital standards. It features a massive Rank S base health pool and Rank S passive regeneration, allowing it to continuously absorb and shrug off sustained battalion-level crossfire.   Flank Vulnerability: The sheer realistic mass and volume of the 5W emitter frame leaves its rear 180-degrees completely exposed and highly fragile. Attacks hitting the rear bypass the primary barrier entirely, striking its Rank D hull directly.Retreat Protocol: If the primary directional shield hits exactly 0%, it permanently shatters. The Aegis Wall immediately aborts its vanguard position and retreats to the nearest active port vessel (Flagship or Battalion Commander) for repairs.
2. Movement & Evasion (Rank D - Super-Heavy Mass)Speed Class: Rank D (Slow / Heavy). Due to its 5W classification, its sheer physical mass makes it sluggish compared to standard combat units.Strafe & Intercept Maneuvering: It locks its rotation to face the heaviest concentration of enemy units. It relies heavily on lateral and reverse thrusters, focusing entirely on spatial positioning rather than forward pursuit.
3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry InteractionDetection Range: Standard visual range (600m).Threat Vectoring: Uses the Fleet Array telemetry to calculate the physical firing lines between known enemy hostiles and its anchored friendly units, updating its intercept vector in real-time.
4. Utility Systems (Defense Output: Rank S / Combat Dmg: Rank D)Primary System: 180-Degree Directional Energy Shield Emitter.   Physical Presence: The projected shield extends incredibly wide, and the 5W hull itself is massive. It creates a physical collision box large enough to cast a protective "shadow" over an entire squad or multiple medium-weight units.   Combat Damage: Rank D (Zero Offensive Weapons / 0 DPS).   
5. AI Loop, Squad & Battalion MechanicsDynamic Intercept AI (The Active Shield): Unlike standard escort anchors, the squad does not adjust its pathing to follow the Aegis Wall. The assigned squad anchors together and paths normally as a standard group. The Aegis Wall operates dynamically around them, constantly calculating enemy firing vectors and actively thrusting to position its massive 5W frame directly between its moving allies and the enemy hostiles.Battalion Linchpin: The Aegis Wall is designed to make or break large-scale engagements. In a full Battalion formation (4 combat squads + 1 Battalion Commander acting as a secondary port/repair vessel), the Wall acts as the primary vanguard, absorbing the alpha-strike of enemy fleets while the 4 squads maneuver freely behind it to return fire.The "Buddy" Protocol (Heavy Escort): If unassigned to a large formation, it automatically pairs with the slowest, highest-value friendly unit in the sector, actively blocking its vulnerable angles.
