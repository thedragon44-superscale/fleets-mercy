Unit Specs: Command Dreadnought (Flagship)
Role: Mobile Command Center, Fleet Spawner, and Primary Win/Loss Condition

1. Vitality & Mitigation

Total Core Hull: 7,500 HP

Quadrant Hull: 1,875 HP per side (Front, Rear, Port, Starboard).

Total Shields: 10,000 HP (2,500 per quadrant).

Dreadnought Armor: 95% flat damage mitigation applied to all incoming fire only after a quadrant's shield has been stripped.

Shield Mechanics: Shields absorb 100% of incoming damage before the hull is touched. They actively regenerate over time as long as the shield has at least 1 HP. If a quadrant's shield hits exactly 0, it "shatters" and cannot regenerate for the remainder of the match.

2. Movement & Evasion

Base Speed: Cruising velocity of 1.0.

Thruster Boost (Spacebar): Increases forward speed up to 2.5x for evasive maneuvers.

Engine Degradation: The boost multiplier scales linearly with the health of the Rear Hull. (e.g., at 1,875 HP you get 2.5x; at 937 HP you get 1.75x).

3. Subsystem Destruction (0% Quadrant Thresholds)

Core Hull (0%): The sum total of your ship reaches 0. Triggers catastrophic ship destruction (Match Lost).

Front Hull (0%): Main Cannon is permanently disabled. Triggers catastrophic ship destruction (Match Lost).

Rear Hull (0%): Main engines and lateral thrusters are critically damaged. Thruster Boost (Spacebar) is permanently disabled, locking the ship to its base 1.0 cruising speed. Lateral strafing (A/D keys) efficiency is crippled by 80%, severely limiting evasive maneuvers.

Starboard / Right Hull (0%): Deployment Bay offline. You can no longer spawn units from your reserves into the battlefield.

Port / Left Hull (0%): Docking Bay offline. You can no longer recall deployed units back to the ship for repairs or reserve refunds.

4. Primary Weapon: Piercing Core Beam

Firing Mechanic: A continuous, sustained raycast of energy rather than discrete physical bullets. Sweeps dynamically, following your mouse cursor in real-time as the ship rotates.

Range: 2,000m (extends well past normal visual range into the Radar Fog).

Damage Output: ~1,000 DPS (applied incrementally per server tick). Capable of instantly vaporizing a standard 150 HP light fighter in roughly 0.15 seconds.

Duration & Cooldown: Fires continuously for exactly 3 seconds. Requires a 10-second cooldown between bursts.

Overpenetration: The beam applies damage continuously to everything touching the raycast line. If a unit dies, it instantly melts and the beam immediately begins damaging the units directly behind it.

Recall Protocols: Manual recall commands from the commander are restricted strictly to Squads, Battalions, and Harvesting Units. Individual standard combat units cannot be manually recalled.
