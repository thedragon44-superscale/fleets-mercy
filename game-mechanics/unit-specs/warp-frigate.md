Unit Specs: Relay Warp Frigate (5W)
Role: Super-Heavy Strategic Jump Anchor, Mobile Wormhole Generator & Fleet Deployment Nexus

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: Rank S / Hull: Rank B)

Health Class: Rank S Shields / Rank B Hull (Unified Pool).

Durability: Protected by a massive, high-capacity energy shield grid designed to absorb heavy combat pressure while maintaining spatial anchoring stability. Its structural hull is reinforced to withstand the extreme gravitational stress of localized wormhole generation.

Retreat Protocol: As a super-heavy stationary anchor, it does not retreat. If its shields drop and its hull is critically compromised, it collapses inward, permanently closing any active wormholes and warp channels it is maintaining.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank B in Transit / Rank D when Anchored)

Speed Class: Rank B while in transit to its designated destination. Once it arrives and locks onto its target Recon Drone, it shifts to Rank D (Stationary / Extremely Slow), anchoring its 5W mass directly to the local spatial grid.

3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry Interaction

Detection Range: Extended radar/telemetry range (1,500m), functioning as a secondary tactical relay node that feeds structural data back to the Flagship through the Fleet Array.

4. Utility Systems & Resource Mechanics (Warp Generation: Rank S / Combat Dmg: Rank D)

Primary System: Quantum Wormhole Generator & Stabilizer.

Resource Cache & Supply Chain:

Each warp transit or spatial jump consumes 1 Warp Canister (synthesized from a combination of Graviton Core, Isotope, and Ore).

The Frigate holds a maximum internal cache of 10 Warp Canisters, which must be actively replenished by incoming Supply Tenders from the fleet's logistics network.

Combat Damage: Rank D (Unarmed / 0 DPS).

5. AI Loop, Routing & Inter-Spatial Mechanics

Destination Anchoring (Recon Tether):

When deployment is initiated, player mouse movement and Flagship direction-changing are temporarily locked.

Recon Drones on the radar/minimap are slightly enlarged and exaggerated. A radial wheel appears, prompting the player to select an active Recon Drone.

The highlighted Recon unit pulses brightly on the radar. Once selected, the Frigate vectors toward it at Rank B speed.

Once the warp zone reaches that drone’s 600m visual range, it stops and remains permanently stationary relative to that location.

Frontline Squad/Battalion Deployment:

Individual units cannot deploy directly to a warp gate.

To deploy a full squad or battalion directly to the front lines, the player holds Right-Click while pressing the deployment key. This routes the deployment straight to the warp gate closest to the enemy.

Canister Validation & Emergency Evacuation:

If a unit is triggered to retreat and a Relay Warp is physically closer to it than any active Command or Port vessel, the retreating unit will use the warp gate to safely evacuate back to port.

Dry-Dock State: If the Frigate's internal Warp Canister cache drops to 0, it is deemed unstable. It is automatically removed as a viable route for retreating units and greyed out/removed from manual warp selection wheels until a Supply Tender arrives to replenish its canisters.

Manual Flagship Jump Protocol & Fleet Tethering:

Holding Right-Click and pressing Left-Click overrides the standard individual unit radial wheel, switching the UI to a specialized radial wheel used to select an active, fueled Relay Warp gate.

Selecting a gate here initiates the Flagship's manual warp sequence, consuming the required Warp Canisters to rip the super-heavy capital ship across the map directly to the chosen Frigate anchor.

Fleet Towing: When the Flagship warps, all units currently anchored, docked, or paired to it are pulled through the spatial fold, warping alongside the capital ship to ensure the fleet stays unified.
