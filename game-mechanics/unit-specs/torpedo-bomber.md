Unit Specs: Heavy Torpedo Bomber (4W)
Role: Capital-Cracking Siege Platform & Infrastructure Destroyer

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: Rank C / Hull: Rank S)

Health Class: Rank C Shields / Rank S Hull (Unified Pool).

Durability & Armor Thresholds: Wrapped in an immensely thick capital hull (Rank S) with thin defensive screens (Rank C).

Vulnerability & Sniper/Flagship Logic: Normal units struggle to scratch its Rank S hull. However, once its C-rank shields are busted, Sniper units (like Lancers) and Flagships can ruthlessly melt the exposed hull.

Retreat Protocol: Disengages to seek the nearest active port or flagship drydock when compromised.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank C - Heavy Cruising Speed)

Speed Class: Rank C (Slow Acceleration / Measured Inertia). Requires dedicated spacing and clear approach vectors.

3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry Interaction

Detection Range: Standard visual/telemetry range (600m). Relies on external telemetry (Recon, Phantom relay beacons, or Command Ship data) for out-of-sight targeting.

4. Utility Systems & Resource Mechanics (Ordnance Output: Rank A / Finite Explosive Ammunition)

Primary System: Heavy Torpedo Bays.

Firing Pattern & Cooldown: Burst-Fire Salvo. Does not fire continuously. Instead, it unleashes a rapid, concentrated burst of a few torpedoes at a time, followed by a strict 2-second cooldown between volleys to cycle the loading mechanisms.

Shield/Hull Damage Interaction: Deals low shield damage, requiring shields to be worn down or stripped first. Once shields are busted, its high-explosive payloads inflict devastating structural hull damage.

Payload & Resupply: Uses finite ammunition. Refilling its payload cache requires a small, balanced mix of ore, isotopes, and plasma gas supplied by Supply Tenders. (Deep economic rules will be locked in during the next development phase).

5. AI Loop & Squad Mechanics

Squad Integration: Operates in heavy strike squadrons or attached to the Battalion Command Ship.

Bomb-Run AI Loop: Plots an approach vector, executes a rapid burst-fire torpedo salvo, and handles the 2-second cooldown cycle while managing its momentum and looking to loop back for resupply.
