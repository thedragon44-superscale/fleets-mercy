Unit Specs: Ion Disabler (1W)
Role: Shield Striker, Subsystem Suppressor & Heavy Hitter Wingman ("Buddy")

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: Rank C / Hull: Rank D)

Health Class: Rank C Shields / Rank D Hull (Unified Pool).

Durability: Lightly protected. Relies on depleting enemy firepower and shields before taking direct counter-fire.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank B - Fast)

Speed Class: Rank B

Agility: High acceleration and responsive thrusters, allowing it to rapidly vector toward enemy capital ships or heavy escorts alongside its assigned lead unit.

3. Vision & Radar Interaction

Detection Range: Standard visual range (600m). Relies on Recon Probes, the Fleet Array, or its "Buddy" unit to acquire targets through Radar Fog.

4. Weapons & Offense (Shield Dmg: Rank S / Hull Dmg: Rank D / Fire Rate: Rank C)

Primary System: Focused Pulsed Ion Cannon

Shield Damage: Rank S (Melts energy shields almost instantly).

Hull Damage: Rank D (Negligible physical impact; incapable of destroying structural plating efficiently on its own).

Disruptor Effect: Continuous hits temporarily freeze the target’s shield regeneration timer and disable secondary weapon turrets for 2 seconds.

Range: 500m

5. AI Attack Pattern, Squad Mechanics & "Buddy" System

Squad Targeting Priority: Scans its assigned squad for the unit with the highest Hull Damage output. The Disabler automatically targets the specific enemy currently locked on by that squadmate, peeling off its shields before the heavy hull damage arrives.

The "Buddy" Protocol: If deployed outside of a squad or if it becomes the sole surviving combatant in its squad, the Disabler enters "Buddy Mode." It automatically pairs with the unit possessing the highest Hull Damage in the entire fleet, shadowing its movements and stripping shields off its target.

Approach Behavior: Employs a Jousting / Boom-and-Zoom vector alongside its squad leader or "Buddy," charging in at max speed to unleash concentrated Ion salvos before circling wide.

Retreat Protocol: None. As a 1W unit, once deployed, it fights to the death to support its squad or "Buddy."
