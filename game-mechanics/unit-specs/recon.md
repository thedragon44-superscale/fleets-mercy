Unit Specs: Recon Probe (1W)
Role: Passive Forward Observer, Fleet Uplink, and Topographical Scout

1. Vitality & Mitigation

Total Hull: 20 (Unified Pool)

Total Shields: 20 (Unified Pool)

Mechanics: Unarmored and extremely fragile. Dies instantly to almost any direct hit.

2. Movement & Evasion

Speed: 3.5 (The absolute fastest unit in the fleet, easily outpacing interceptors and standard projectiles).

Agility: Maximum lateral acceleration. Can reverse direction almost instantly to evade incoming fire.

3. Weapons & Offense

Projectile: None (Unarmed).

Damage: 0.

4. Vision, Radar Fog & HUD UI (Primary Mechanic)

Detection Range: 2,500m (Over 4x the standard visual range).

UI - Radar Bubble: The probe's 2,500m detection radius is subtly highlighted on the player's HUD radar, showing exactly what areas of the map are currently illuminated.

UI - Obstacle Rendering: Environmental obstacles and celestial bodies illuminated by the probe are drawn on the radar as approximate geometric shapes matching their actual physical footprint on the map.

UI - Enemy Rendering: Enemy units detected within the bubble appear on the radar not as generic dots, but as distinct icons representing their specific unit weight class symbol (1W, 2W, 3W, etc.).

Economic & Military Uplink: Resource harvesting units rely on this telemetry to pathfind to nodes. Combat units use it to calculate cover and pathing, and to acquire target locks through the fog.

5. AI Flight Pattern & Strategy

Behavior (Blind Sweep & Anchor): Upon deployment, the Probe flies outward in a sweeping search pattern through the Radar Fog. It only reacts once an entity breaches its 2,500m detection bubble.

Orbiting & Permanent Anchors: Once a target (enemy ship or celestial body) is found, it anchors itself, maintaining a distant, high-speed orbit (~1,800m away). If an anchored enemy ship is destroyed, it acquires a new target within range. Environmental obstacles and resource nodes are permanent anchors; even if depleted, the physical structure remains, and the Probe will continue to illuminate it for the fleet.

Evasion Protocol: If an enemy unit closes within 1,000m, the Probe overrides its orbit and flees directly away at maximum speed until a safe distance is re-established.

Retreat Protocol: None. Fights (scouts) to the death.
