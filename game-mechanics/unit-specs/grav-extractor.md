Unit Specs: Grav Extractor (2W)
Role: Unarmed Industrial Harvester (3 of 3 Resource Collectors)

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Hull: Rank B / Shields: Rank C)

Health Class: Rank B Hull / Rank C Shields (Unified Pool).

Durability: Heavily reinforced gravitational damping frame designed to handle high-density gravity wells and crystal/isotopic harvesting.

Retreat Protocol: If its shields are completely broken (0%), it immediately aborts operations and retreats to the Flagship's Port (Left) Docking Bay for repairs and shelter.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank D - Slow & Heavy)

Speed Class: Rank D

Stealth Pathing AI: On both outward and inward journeys, the Extractor uses friendly Recon Probe telemetry to plot vectors through asteroid fields and around environmental obstacles, actively avoiding enemy line-of-sight and combat zones.

Evasive Maneuvers: None. It does not perform panic evasive maneuvers or flee when taking fire; it remains 100% focused on its task until its shields break completely.

3. Vision, Radar & Fleet Array Interaction

Detection Range: Standard visual range (600m).

Recon Telemetry Dependency: Incapable of locating or pathfinding to gravity wells or exotic crystal deposits in Radar Fog without active Fleet Array telemetry provided by a Recon Probe.

4. Mining Systems & Resource Classification

Harvesting Specialization: Operates as 3 of 3 specialized 2W harvesting ships. Extracts Resource Type 5 (Exotic Crystals) and Resource Type 6 (Graviton Cores / Rare Isotopes).

Primary Tool: Gravitational Resonance Pulser / Tether.

Extraction Rate: Rank S (Rapidly fractures dense crystals and stabilizes heavy isotopes).

Combat Damage: Rank D (Negligible).

5. AI Loop, Squad Mechanics & "Buddy" Defense Anchor

Squad/Battalion Anchor: If assigned to a squad or battalion, the Grav Extractor acts as the primary movement anchor. The entire squad/battalion enters a dedicated escort formation around the Extractor, prioritizing its protection and following its vector to and from harvesting sites.

Harvesting Loop:

Acquires illuminated node (for Exotic Crystals / Graviton Cores) via Fleet Array telemetry.

Paths stealthily to the node using obstacle cover (escort squad following).

Extracts until cargo hold reaches 100% capacity.

Returns to the Flagship's Port Docking Bay to deposit materials.

Harvester Transit Optimization: Harvesters at full capacity prioritize offloading directly to the nearest Supply Tender in the field, returning immediately to harvesting locations afterward. Harvesters only travel back to the Flagship/Battalion Command Ship if carrying capital-exclusive construction resources, requiring hull/shield repairs, or if manually recalled.
