Unit Specs: Viper Interceptor (1W)
Role: Light Dogfighter / Swarm Corral Unit

1. Vitality & Mitigation

Total Hull: 50 (Unified Pool)

Total Shields: 100 (Unified Pool)

Mechanics: No directional quadrants. Shields take 100% of damage until depleted, then damage bleeds to hull.

2. Movement & Evasion

Speed: 2.8 (Extremely fast; outpaces the Flagship's max thruster boost).

Agility: Very high turn rate and lateral acceleration, allowing for rapid directional changes.

3. Weapons & Offense

Projectile: Twin Light Lasers

Damage: 10 per hit

Fire Rate: Fast (fires every 0.5 seconds)

Range: 400m

4. Vision & Radar Fog

Detection Range: 600m (Standard visual screen range. Relies on Recon units to spot targets further out).

5. AI Attack Pattern & Strategy

Behavior (Corral / "Cut the Pie"): Vipers fly in at maximum speed until they reach weapons range, then aggressively strafe in a semi-circle directly in front of the enemy's forward trajectory. This creates a physical wall of swarm fighters, boxing the enemy in and preventing forward advancement.

Retreat Protocol: None. As a 1W unit, the Viper fights to the death to maintain the corral.
