Unit Specs: Aegis Repair Corvette (2W)Role: In-Flight Field Maintenance, Shattered Shield Restoration & Combat Triage   
1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: Rank A / Hull: Rank D)Health Class: Rank A Shields / Rank D Hull (Unified Pool).Durability: Features an oversized, high-capacity shield generator to protect itself while performing delicate field operations, but possesses an extremely fragile physical frame.   Retreat & Dedication Protocol: The Aegis never retreats due to low health. It remains permanently with its anchored unit, squad, battalion, or assigned port ship, fighting (and healing) to the death. It only breaks formation when its resource cache is completely depleted.
2. Movement & Evasion (Rank C - Moderate)Speed Class: Rank C (Medium Speed).   Tactical Evasion (Meat-Shielding): Highly stabilized vectoring allows it to constantly orbit its anchored target. The Aegis actively maneuvers to position friendly units directly between itself and known enemy combatants, using its heavily armored squadmates as physical cover while it works.
3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry InteractionDetection Range: Standard visual range (600m).Closed-Loop Telemetry: Unlike global support ships, the Aegis operates on a strict localized network. It only monitors the real-time structural and shield data of the specific squad, battalion, "buddy," or port ship it is currently anchored to. It is blind to the triage needs of the wider fleet.
4. Utility Systems & Resource Consumption (Repair: Rank S / Combat: Rank D)Primary System: Dual Nanite Repair Beams.   Mechanics: Fires two continuous energy tethers that actively restore depleted Hull HP and reconstruct broken/cracked shields (bringing shields that have been permanently shattered at 0 HP back online so they can regenerate).Resource Dependency: Healing is not free; the beams consume physical resources from the Aegis's internal cache:Standard Repairs: Restoring standard unit hulls and broken shields requires Metal.Capital Subsystems: Repairing critical Capital/Port Ship subsystems (specifically crippled Thrusters) requires a complex weave of Metal, Exotic Crystals, and Rare Isotopes.Combat Damage: Rank D (Unarmed / 0 DPS).
5. AI Loop & Logistics DependencyAnchored Triage: Trails slightly behind its anchored frontline, continuously dumping resources to keep its squad's hulls patched and shattered shields cycling back online.The Resupply Loop: When its internal cache runs dry, the Aegis temporarily breaks its anchor and scans the Fleet Array for the nearest Supply Tender carrying the necessary materials. It vectors to the Tender, refits its cache, and immediately paths back to resume its post with its anchored units.
