Unit Specs: Vortex Minelayer (3W)
Role: Heavy Spatial Chokepoint Control, Area Denial & Gravity Disruption

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: Rank B / Hull: Rank B)

Health Class: Rank B Shields / Rank B Hull (Unified Pool).

Durability: Built on a heavy 3W flat-disc chassis with reinforced plating to survive frontline escort duty while laying down spatial traps.

Retreat Protocol: Follows standard fleet retreat rules. If its health drops to critical thresholds, it breaks away to the nearest active port vessel (Flagship or Battalion Commander) for repairs.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank C - Moderate)

Speed Class: Rank C (Medium Speed).

Inertial Drift Maneuvering: Omnidirectional thrusters allow it to strafe and rotate independently of its travel vector, keeping its deployment chutes facing the vector of advance.

3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry Interaction

Detection Range: Standard visual range (600m).

Telemetry Link: Tracks active mine fields and relays spatial data back to the Fleet Array.

4. Utility Systems & Resource Mechanics (Mine Deployment: Rank S / Combat Dmg: Rank C to Shields / 0 to Hull)

Primary System: Graviton Implosion Mine System.

Payload & Resupply: Comes equipped with a massive internal cache of 50 Graviton Mines to start.

Material Requirements: Manufacturing and reloading mines requires a precise blend of Graviton Cores, Metal, and Plasma Gas.

Resupply Loop: When its payload cache is depleted, it seeks out the nearest Supply unit carrying the required resources to reload. If no Supply unit is available in the network, it automatically falls back to the Flagship to rearm.

Damage Output: Rank C damage to shields / 0 damage to hull. The detonation actively fries energy shielding while leaving physical ship structures untouched by direct blasts. (Note: There is no friendly fire in the game, so mines will never affect allied vessels).

5. AI Loop, Recon Anchoring & Vortex Mechanics

Recon Anchoring: The minelayer is anchored directly to active Recon Drones rather than a standard combat squad.

Predictive Sowing: It constantly patrols between its anchored Recon unit and known enemy positions, liberally sowing staggered lines of graviton mines directly in the path between the recon sensor and incoming enemy forces.

Graviton Vortex & Pull Mechanics: Upon proximity trigger, the mine initiates a violent spatial implosion lasting approximately 5 seconds. Throughout this window, it actively sucks enemy units inward toward the center of the mine, disrupting their movement vectors and grouping them tightly together.

Timed Phantom Revelation: The intense gravitational and energetic distortion breaks stealth field generators. Any Phantom units caught in the vortex are forced out of stealth and explicitly revealed to radar telemetry for the exact 5-second duration of the active detonation (provided they are within active recon drone range).
