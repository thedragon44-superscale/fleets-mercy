Unit Specs: Command Escort Ship (3W)
Role: Dedicated Capital Bodyguard, Interception Screen, & Vanguard Shield

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Shields: Rank B / Hull: Rank B)

Health Class: Rank B Shields / Rank B Hull (Unified Pool).

Durability & Positioning: Balanced resilience designed to take the brunt of incoming hostile attention away from high-value command vessels.

Retreat Protocol: Follows standard fleet retreat rules, disengaging to seek the nearest active port or flagship drydock when its health reaches critical thresholds.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank B - Constant Frigate Speed / No Thruster Scaling)

Speed Class: Rank B (Unflinching Cruise Speed).

Thruster Disconnect Rule: While it stays glued to its command vessel during normal operations, it cannot match the speed of a flagship thruster boost. If a flagship or command ship initiates an emergency boost, the escort ships cannot accelerate; they maintain their steady Rank B speed and must diligently catch up to the capital ship post-burn.

Tactical Positioning Loop: Anchors within a standard squad range radius around its assigned command vessel, positioning its hull directly between the protected capital ship and the highest concentration of enemy units.

3. Vision, Radar & Telemetry Interaction

Radar Relay Role: Functions as a passive radar relay for the fleet sensor array, extending telemetry around its protected command vessel.

Engagement Range: Hard-capped firing and engagement range of 1200m.

4. Utility Systems & Resource Mechanics (Ordnance Output: Rank B / Infinite Ammo / Heat Management)

Primary System: Medium Point-Defense & Interception Batteries. (No friendly fire applies).

Ammunition Model: Infinite Ammunition, balanced via a strict thermal/cooldown cycle.

Fire Rate & Thermal Penalty:

Fire Rate: Rank B standard release cadence.

Cycling Rule: Fires continuously for 3 seconds, followed immediately by a mandatory 1-second cooldown period before weapons can cycle again.

5. AI Loop & Deployment Behavior

Deployment Restriction: Cannot be deployed in a standard squad. Built exclusively as an independent escort asset.

Assignment Logic:

Deployed individually -> Automatically assigns to protect the Flagship.

Deployed in a battalion setup -> Automatically assigns to protect the Battalion Command Ship.

Bodyguard AI Loop: Matches command ship movement, intercepting incoming threats and interposing itself along the vector of highest enemy concentration.
