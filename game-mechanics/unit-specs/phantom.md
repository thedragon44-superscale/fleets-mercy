Unit Specs: Phantom Transport (1W)
Role: Stealth Infiltrator & Recon Hunter

1. Vitality & Mitigation (Rank B - Moderate)

Health Class: Rank B

Health Pool: Unified Hull & Shield Pool. Possesses moderate durability for a 1W unit, allowing it to absorb retaliatory fire after revealing itself to eliminate its target.

2. Movement & Evasion (Rank C - Moderate)

Speed Class: Rank C

Agility: Measured and deliberate. Relies on tactical positioning and line-of-sight concealment rather than raw speed to approach targets.

3. Stealth & Visibility Mechanics (Primary System)

Radar Immunity: Completely immune to radar detection under standard conditions. Recon Probes cannot detect or illuminate a Phantom on the HUD radar or fleet array at any distance.

Visual Detection: Only becomes visible on the visual screen / fleet array when entering strict visual range (600m) of an enemy unit or structure.

Decloak Triggers: Its radar shadow is broken and it becomes fully visible on radar and HUD screens if it takes damage, discharges weapons, or drops a payload.

Cloak Recovery: Automatically recovers full radar immunity after 5 consecutive seconds of not taking damage, not firing weapons, and not dropping payloads.

4. Weapons & Offense (Damage: Rank C / Fire Rate: Rank D)

Primary System: Anti-System Pulse Cannon / Sabotage Charge

Damage: Rank C against both Shields and Hull.

Fire Rate: Rank D (Slow, heavy cycle time favoring burst ambush strikes over continuous fire).

Range: Point-blank / Short range (requires closing in before revealing its position).

5. AI Attack Pattern & Strategy ("Recon Hunter")

Targeting Priority: Actively seeks out and targets enemy Recon Probes painted by the friendly fleet array.

Approach Behavior: Uses environmental obstacles (asteroids, structures) and stays outside of enemy sightlines to remain completely undetected while stalking its target.

Ambush Protocol: Because it remains invisible to radar, enemy Recon Probes cannot trigger their evasion or flee until the Phantom actively fires its weapon, takes damage, or drops a payload.

Retreat Protocol: None. As a 1W unit, once engaged, it fights to the death to blind the enemy fleet.
