# Universe Lore & Historical Archive: Fleet’s Mercy

## 1. The Cradle Collapse and the Great Diaspora (~1,000 Years Ago)
Humanity’s interstellar story began on **Solaria-Prime**, the historical cradle world. Centuries of unchecked industrialization, ecological neglect, and hyper-consumption pushed the planet to its absolute breaking point. The planetary crust was mined to exhaustion, and the upper atmosphere became a toxic, smog-choked cage. 

As global food production collapsed and resources dwindled, devastating resource wars erupted across the surface among massive corporate and political conglomerates. Recognizing that the cradle world could no longer sustain human life, the unified global authorities—the early precursor to the **Interplanetary United Nations (IUN)**—initiated the **Great Diaspora**. Massive colony arks and sleeper ships were launched into the deep void to seed new worlds, leaving Solaria-Prime to slowly age into an over-bureaucratic administrative hub attempting to police a rapidly fracturing human diaspora.

## 2. The Spreading Waves of Settlement
As humanity spilled out across the stars, different groups settled worlds molded by vastly different environments, giving rise to specialized human sub-species and distinct economic doctrines over centuries of isolation:

* **Thera-Voss (~850 Years Ago):** Settled early in the diaspora wave, Thera-Voss is a high-gravity, hyper-dense terrestrial world with massive subterranean tectonic plates and crushing surface pressure. Generations of living under intense gravitational forces forged the **Voss-Born**—shorter, dense-muscled humans with reinforced bone structures. Culturally utilitarian and disciplined, Thera-Voss became the heavy-industrial heartland of human space, driven by corporate-national syndicates specializing in heavy manufacturing, ballistic military assets, and deep-crust extraction.
* **Aethelgard (~600 Years Ago):** Discovered during the middle expansion era, Aethelgard is a gas super-giant with no solid landmass, encircled by permanent high-altitude plasma storms and deep nebula belts. Civilization here evolved entirely within tethered aerostats and floating orbital platforms. Its inhabitants, the **Cirrus-Kin**, became tall, slender, and lightweight with hyper-efficient cardiovascular systems. Culturally decentralized, the Cloud Leagues specialized in agile, high-altitude harvesting of volatile gases, isotopes, and rare materials.

## 3. The Lost Monolith: Vesper-Crown (>1,500 Years Ago / Discovered 20 Years Ago)
Long before Solaria-Prime collapsed, a massive, ultra-secretive sleeper ship carrying political dissidents, royals, and elite pre-diaspora scientists drifted far off course, ultimately discovering and settling **Vesper-Crown**—a breathtaking, resource-rich world surrounded by vibrant natural rings and moons. 

Completely cut off from Earth, they spent over a thousand years in total isolation. They evolved into a true societal monolith governed by an absolute, divine-right monarch and a rigid aristocratic caste. In their historical texts, humanity was born right there under the Crown; they had zero knowledge of Solaria-Prime or the Great Diaspora. 

When their deep-space exploratory vessels finally pushed past their system's borders two decades ago, they expected to find empty stars or gods. Instead, they collided with the sprawling, chaotic web of corporate trade routes, IUN bureaucracy, and outer-rim conflict.

---

## 4. The Contemporary Era: The Gilded Adjustment & The Four-Way Theater
The modern timeline is set approximately twenty years after Vesper-Crown’s emergence onto the interstellar stage. The shock of first contact has given way to cold, calculated ambition as the Monarchy rapidly industrialized, studied foreign designs, and built custom royal variations of all standard fleet architectures.

Today, human space is locked in a volatile four-way geopolitical scramble under the shadow of the **Interplanetary United Nations (IUN)**:

* **Vesper-Crown:** The proud, pristine newcomers armed with fully realized, ornate military doctrines, viewing the rest of humanity as fractured, barbaric republics ripe for divine reclamation.
* **Thera-Voss:** The heavy-industrial powerhouse fighting a brutal war of attrition to secure critical resource nodes and hazard-rich territories against outer-world encroachment.
* **Aethelgard:** The elusive Cloud Leagues waging an asymmetric, nebula-based defense to protect their atmospheric sovereignty and vital isotopic harvesting loops from foreign exploitation.
* **Solaria-Prime (The IUN):** The ancient cradle-world and bureaucratic center desperately trying to enforce its supreme interplanetary authority over a galaxy tearing itself apart under the brutal realities of **Fleet’s Mercy**.
