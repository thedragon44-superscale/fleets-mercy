Macro-Resource Master Spec Sheet
1. Metal
Harvesting Vessel: Mining Barge (2W)

Extraction Sources: Asteroid belts, rocky planetary crusts, and dense mineral moons.

Logistics & Storage: Hauled by Supply Tenders (3W) to active command nodes, drydocks, and repair staging areas.

Unit & Payload Dependencies:

Aegis Repair Units (2W): Consumed directly to execute structural hull repairs in the field.

Battalion Command Ships & Flagships (5W): Utilized exclusively for automated station-keeping and dockyard repairs.

General Fleet: Serves as the primary baseline material for all base and unit construction.

2. Ore
Harvesting Vessel: Mining Barge (2W)

Extraction Sources: Heavy ore-rich asteroids, tectonic planetary crust deposits, and rocky celestial bodies.

Logistics & Storage: Hauled in bulk by Supply Tenders (3W) to heavy manufacturing nodes and fortification sites.

Unit & Payload Dependencies:

Defensive Gun Emplacements (4W): Paired directly with Graviton Cores as the heavy physical mass required to manufacture and restock their stationary artillery ammunition caches.

3. Isotopes
Harvesting Vessel: Plasma Skimmer (2W) / Isotope Extraction Variant

Extraction Sources: Gas giant upper atmospheres, volatile nebulae, and ice moon thermal vents.

Logistics & Storage: Managed by Supply Tenders (3W) to power high-tier industrial grids and fleet infrastructure.

Unit & Payload Dependencies:

Torpedo Bombers: Consumed as high-energy chemical/nuclear propellant and payload fuel for heavy long-range missile and torpedo munitions.

Propulsion & Power Systems: Required for advanced ship drives, heavy power grids, and fleet thruster-boosting mechanics.

4. Plasma Gas
Harvesting Vessel: Plasma Skimmer (2W)

Extraction Sources: Deep-nebula pockets, highly pressurized gas vents, and stellar radiation clouds.

Logistics & Storage: Transported under strict containment by Supply Tenders (3W) to tactical manufacturing nodes.

Unit & Payload Dependencies:

Cryo-Flak Frigates (3W): Combined with metal to manufacture, maintain, and restock their area-denial cryo-shell payloads.

Energetic Systems: Powers high-intensity plasma weapon frameworks and explosive propulsion compounds.

5. Graviton Cores
Harvesting Vessel: Grav Extractor (2W)

Extraction Sources: Rare, high-density space-time anomalies, core singularity nodes, and deep planetary rift deposits.

Logistics & Storage: High-priority, low-volume cargo hauled securely by Supply Tenders (3W) to strategic locations.

Unit & Payload Dependencies:

Warp Units: Consumed as the fundamental energy-mass catalyst required to warp or teleport fleet assets across spatial nodes.

Defensive Gun Emplacements (4W): Paired with ore to fuel high-tier, space-shattering artillery ammunition caches.
